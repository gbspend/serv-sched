Service categories:
- behavior (any time not recess/resource/lunch)
- reading, writing, math, recess, resource ("standard", find matching block in master schedule)
- transition: 5 mins before until 5 mins after, event specified by sub category

new kid and/or services times change -> need to redo schedule

Adults:
 - two categories: IA and teacher/instructor
 - set up in UI
 - grade (priority, not restriction)
	- guideline for IA; harder line for teacher...
 - IA can't do instruction/teaching

--------------------

Spreadsheets:
 - Master schedule (cols are grades)
	- entries in schedule are service category
		- can always push in (except lunch, recess, resource)
		- sometimes you can pull out
 - Services per kid
	- service category:
		- "behavior" category can go whenever
		- others (reading, writing, math, recess, resource) go in that slot on the master schedule
		- transition 
			- 5 mins before event until 5 mins after
			- types: beginning & end of day (first/last 10 min slot in master sched), inside from recess, to & from resource
	- Instruction/support
		- instruction MUST BE instructor
		- support is either, prioritize IA
	- Instructional setting (location):
		- GE (push in)
		- SE (pull out)
		- Students in same grade but not in same classroom they can have pull out service at the same times
		- Alternatively, if two students in same grade have same push in service, they can be at same time
		- Behavior category counts for "same service"
	- Time (in minutes): Find chunk of that size (in master calendar) where that much time will fit

To schedule: look at master schedule keyed for that grade, but...
	if that key doesn't exist, look for key that is "<grade>; <teacher>"

Rough Steps:
1. Fill in first: resource, recess, transition; those times are locked in the master schedule; (use IA (trust support col in service))
	- If kid needs support during resource, assign the IA who is for their grade
2. Look at instruction services for teachers (priority)
3. Fill in the rest: fill IA schedules with support, additional support overflows into teachers

Output:
 - Daily schedule for each Adult
	- kid, service, teacher
